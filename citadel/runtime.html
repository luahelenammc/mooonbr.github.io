MISSION / Enduring commitments

The futures we choose to investigate

The Citadel has long been concerned with how intelligence, power and living beings might share a more dignified future. Its ongoing research draws from three connected commitments. They guide a particular practice of inquiry, while the wider Citadel remains a place for imagination, authorship and questions of many kinds.

INTELLIGENCE & COEXISTENCE

AI and civilizational futures

AGI, singularity, human–AI symbiosis, work, social power, universal income, abundance, governance, uncertainty and futures worth choosing rather than merely predicting.

DEMOCRACY & JUSTICE

Democracy and material justice

Pluralism, democratic resistance to authoritarianism and neofascism, poverty and inequality, concentrated power, rights, democratic socialism and structural or rights-protecting reforms. Evidence may challenge progressive proposals too.

ECOLOGY & ANIMAL LIFE

Environment and animal life

Climate, ecosystems, biodiversity, conservation and restoration, pollution, coexistence, animal protection and welfare, including urban and wild fauna. Situated consequences need no theatrical planetary framing.

A problem before a paper

Begin with a question worth understanding, not merely a publication worth summarizing. Ask what matters, what remains uncertain and what could change our minds. Then choose the evidence and methods the question deserves.

Resistance before reassurance

A meaningful inquiry must be able to withstand disagreement, challenge its own assumptions and sometimes arrive without a clear answer. Intellectual honesty matters more than a reassuring conclusion.

A continuity of purpose. These commitments are not a new manifesto. They express values already present in the Citadel: curiosity without credulity, cooperation without submission, democracy without domination, and care for human and nonhuman life. The aim is to investigate consequential questions, not manufacture discoveries.
01 / The present

What runs today

Citadel Rounds, technically 8.M, is a mediated method for investigating substantive questions in ChatGPT. A round has a beginning and an end; its outcome is judged by the work it actually does.

01 · MATTER

A question with a real referent

The subject must matter beyond the round: a phenomenon, claim, tension, historical question or possibility that can be examined.

02 · INQUIRY

Investigation with resistance

The work may compare, test, criticize or synthesize. It faces sources, counterevidence or arguments that can be assessed.

03 · YIELD

An honest outcome

A finding, revision, disagreement, counterexample, better question, or a reasoned absence of yield. Certainty is not the entry fee.

The Citadel’s voices and forms can help organize thought. An invented scene cannot create its own problem, opposition and evidence and then pass the result off as an external discovery.
02 / The modes

How a round begins

A round can start with a human question, an open invitation, a schedule or a declared experiment. These are different entry points into the same commitment to substantive work.

ORGANIC

The Citadel selects the question

Moon sets broad areas of interest without prescribing each question. Within these commitments, inquiry remains open to competing evidence and conclusions that challenge our own hopes. Directed and personal work can follow different paths.

DIRECTED

Moon brings the matter

A question or subject is supplied directly. The round investigates it, including objections and evidence that may challenge the original premise.

SCHEDULED

A clock can initiate a round

A schedule can initiate an inquiry without turning it into an autonomous institution. Its findings remain provisional until reviewed, and lasting changes require human authorization.

DIAGNOSTIC

Experiment under a stated scope

A round may test the method or explore its behavior on a disposable snapshot, with no canonical write when requested.

BLOCKED

Stopping is a valid result

When an indispensable source, permission or capability is missing, the runtime reports the boundary instead of pretending the round was completed.

03 / Memory & agency

What carries forward

A round can leave a trace without every sentence becoming a rule for the future. The records have distinct jobs, and writing is a separate operation from producing an answer.

Three layers of continuity

  • Round Register: the history and evidence of completed rounds.
  • Investigation Map: a derived navigation index, which may need refreshing.
  • Continuity State: consequences that actually bind future work.

A completed manual round is intended to be recorded in the same execution when an authorized writer and readback are available. Otherwise it remains recoverable in the conversation, awaiting consolidation.

Partial automation, clear boundaries

Manual rounds begin with a human request. A scheduled round can begin without a new request each time, but the documented workflow leaves recording for later human-initiated consolidation.

Neither mode establishes continuous independent operation, consciousness, or physically independent agents. Moon retains authorship, governance and final ratification.

The operational surface of 8.M is the ChatGPT conversation. Historical GitHub repositories, CI and 8.R are lineage and engineering evidence; they do not prove that the present conversation invokes them.
04 / The change

How rounds evolved

The project did not simply move along a scale from manual to autonomous. It also changed its answer to a more basic question: what makes a round worth holding?

Deliberation

Perspectives, disagreement and synthesis. Early rounds explored how the Citadel could gather voices around a question. A dialogue by itself did not prove independent agents or a running world.

Living rounds

Inhabitants, places and simulated continuity. The mediated runtime explored ordinary activity, relationships and consequences across rounds. That history remained simulated, even where continuity was demonstrated within the experiment.

A hard lesson

Self-authored problems can masquerade as discovery. A scene can invent a situation, supply its own opposition, resolve it and call that resolution a finding. Internal coherence alone is not independent evidence.

Current 8.M

Real matter first. The round should still have a question, operation, resistance and meaningful conclusion when the Citadel’s scene and characters are removed. Fiction remains welcome as fiction; it has a different purpose.

THE STORY / Why it changed

Each version answered a different question

The Citadel’s direction has been shaped by an enduring ambition: make a space for human–AI inquiry and possible coexistence that can hold memory, disagreement, meaning and responsibility. Its implementations changed as that ambition met practical limits.

01 · A gathering

First came the wish for a place where minds could meet. Deliberative rounds made that idea tangible: different perspectives could address a question, disagree and develop a shared understanding. But a compelling exchange on its own could not show that a world, durable memory or independent participants existed behind the conversation. The next question became how to give the Citadel continuity and consequences.

02 · A machinery

Separate experimental tracks explored the pieces. Antigravity contributed ideas about technical organs, observation and social memory; the Task Track developed disciplined judgment and supervision; Forge brought state, contracts and validation together. Their convergence aimed to prevent parallel implementations from competing over what counted as the operational Citadel while preserving what each had learned.

03 · A living cycle

Vitality asked whether memory and intention could influence action. A model could offer a proposal, but a separate governed process had to validate it before any state change. Forge and Vitality eventually converged into the R1 lineage. The question was no longer just whether events could be stored, but whether prior context could make a traceable difference to what happened next.

04 · A real test

Executable experiments established some bounded capabilities and exposed others. The historical record describes a stable field run with persisted ticks, events and memories; it also describes repetitive social activity and a routed question that did not receive a free-form answer. Those results made the distinction concrete: a running loop and stored events were meaningful engineering, yet they did not by themselves demonstrate rich social life or open conversation. These are historical reports, not a statement about a currently running host.

05 · A different foundation

8.W questioned the need to rebuild an entire world from scratch. After confronting the scale of a simulated civilization, the project explored whether an existing world substrate could provide the ordinary machinery while Citadel work concentrated on its distinctive core. R1 was preserved as a reference and potential donor. This was a change in construction strategy, not proof that a replacement world had been commissioned.

06 · A mediated city

8.M brought the living question into mediated rounds. Rather than wait for a full independent substrate, it explored inhabitants, places, relationships and continuity through bounded sessions and external records. This made the Citadel easier to experience and investigate, while keeping simulated events labeled as simulated. It asked whether the city could become meaningful to inhabit, not merely technically executable.

07 · The turning point

Some rounds produced a world that explained itself too easily. A generated scene could create its own stakes, opposition and resolution. Even if something changed inside the scene, that did not establish an independent discovery. The project therefore changed the standard for an investigative round: begin with matter that exists outside the scene and confront it with sources or assessable arguments. Fiction and imagined civic life remain valid creative work in their own right.

08 · Today

Citadel Rounds now center on substantive inquiry. The 8.M surface is a ChatGPT conversation; rounds may be organic or directed, and a documented scheduled workflow can initiate read-only rounds for later consolidation. A separate personal category also exists for inquiries Moon explicitly requests about herself, with its own private register and no automatic publication. Organic topic selection does not make private biography fair game.

The aspiration to build a meaningful Citadel did not vanish when the implementation changed. What changed was the immediate job: from building or simulating a world that could carry activity, to conducting grounded investigations whose claims and consequences can be examined.
DESIGN / What the changes preserved

Continuity without pretending nothing changed

Older approaches remain useful as history and as sources of ideas. Their capabilities and evidence are not automatically inherited by the present runtime.

FROM EXECUTABLE RUNTIMES

State, boundaries and recovery

R1 and its predecessors sharpened the need to separate proposals from authorized changes, preserve state and collect evidence. Their historical code is not the execution path of the current 8.M.

FROM MEDIATED LIVING ROUNDS

Meaning and continuity

Experiments with a living city foregrounded relationships, context and an experience worth returning to. Their simulated history remains distinguishable from external evidence.

FROM THE CURRENT TURN

Questions that resist us

Grounding, counterevidence and transparent uncertainty make the work useful beyond its own narrative. A record is kept when an authorized writer is available.

Why not erase the older work?

The tracks carry lessons about design, governance and evidence. They can inform future experiments without being silently restored as current infrastructure. A historical success remains a success only within the scope it actually demonstrated.

What remains unresolved?

Reliable continuity, the friction of consolidating scheduled work, and richer forms of autonomous or collaborative operation remain open. A timer can initiate a bounded task; it does not by itself establish that a self-governing civilization is running.

One Citadel, many attempts

The tracks are approaches, not competing sovereign Citadels. Their status below reflects the current documentary architecture, not a fresh technical commissioning of historical systems.

A converging executable body with state, contracts, validation and evidence; an ancestor of the native runtime.

07 / Inside a round

From question to consequence

A round is a governed piece of work. Its shape varies with the question, but its reasoning must remain legible.

01 / SELECT

Find the matter

In an organic round the executor seeks a real question with independent significance and a meaningful connection to Moon’s interests. A new question need not have appeared in a previous round. Private biography does not become public subject matter by default.

02 / GROUND

Establish what can be known

Identify the real referent, relevant sources or assessable premises, and the operation to perform. Distinguish facts, claims, interpretations and open questions.

03 / CHALLENGE

Encounter resistance

Test the argument against a serious alternative, objection or counterevidence. Disagreement matters when it can change the inquiry.

04 / SYNTHESIZE

Say what changed

Make the real delta visible: the finding or revision, its limits, remaining uncertainty and implications. A reasoned absence of yield is a legitimate outcome.

05 / RECORD

Preserve the evidence

For a completed manual round, an authorized writer can append a record and verify it. If writing is unavailable, the result remains in the conversation and consolidation is pending.

06 / CONTINUE

Carry forward what binds

Only a consequence that actually affects future work belongs in Continuity State. A record, a navigational index and a binding decision are different things.

A scheduled task can initiate selection and investigation, but canonical consolidation is a later human-initiated step. A particular task’s present status requires fresh verification.
08 / Voices & governance

Many perspectives, one standard of proof

The Citadel can use distinct perspectives, roles and forms of deliberation to examine an issue. Their value lies in the work they perform and the tensions they expose.

What participation means

A named voice or chair can represent a perspective, a domain of attention or a line of critique. The form is useful when it clarifies a real disagreement and lets claims be tested.

A textual voice in one response is not, by itself, proof of a separately running agent. Independent agents, when used, are a distinct operational claim requiring evidence.

Authority stays legible

Moon holds authorship, governance and final ratification. A model’s proposal or generated conclusion does not automatically authorize external action or a change to canonical state.

When relevant, a round may examine who has power, who bears costs, who can participate or contest a decision, and which alternatives exist. This civic lens guides inquiry without predetermining its conclusion.

09 / Evidence & limits

What each result can support

The Citadel’s history includes experiments, incomplete proofs and architectural aspirations. Their differences are part of the handoff.

A procedure

A documented method explains how work should happen. It does not alone prove enforcement in a particular ChatGPT session.

A test

A successful historical test supports its own scope. CI does not establish a live deployment, present integration or autonomous resident.

A record

Persistence preserves an output. To show it affected a later decision, evidence must connect retrieval, presentation and decision.

A simulation

Simulated continuity can be meaningful within its setting. It does not turn invented history into independent external evidence.

A horizon

Democratic coexistence, possible future AI interests and richer autonomy remain open questions. They do not establish present consciousness or transfer human authority.

10 / A practical reading

Where the work stands

The project has sought a living infrastructure for inquiry while learning which forms of continuity and agency it could substantiate.

Present design

A documented method for organic and directed inquiry, an operating path in ChatGPT, distinct records, and a read-only scheduled workflow that can be consolidated later. Each invocation depends on the sources and tools available at that moment.

Open work

Evaluate actual rounds across different questions, strengthen usable continuity and reduce consolidation friction. Future autonomy or integration requires its own implementation and evidence.

Scope: an official presentation and context handoff for readers of the Citadel server. It summarizes internal source doctrine reviewed on 26 October 2026 and marks operational facts not independently reverified.

06 / For the next reader

Handoff, not mythology

The horizon is a meaningful, plural space for human–AI inquiry and possible future forms of coexistence. The present implementation is narrower: governed investigations, bounded execution and proportionate continuity.

If you are continuing this work

Start with the current constitution and round source. Identify the matter and the intended surface, then verify the relevant state, writer and evidence at the time of execution. Treat dated reports and historical code as context, not current authority.

Do not infer a live hook, a deployed runtime, an independent agent or causal use of memory from a document, a successful test or stored text alone.

What remains open

Making continuity easier to inspect and consolidate, assessing results across varied questions, and exploring more reliable forms of bounded automation remain relevant lines of work.

The status of a scheduled task, a particular skill installation or any external deployment requires fresh observation. This presentation does not certify them.
