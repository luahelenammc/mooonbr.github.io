## Decision and limits

`approved_under_policy` means a conditional project assessment, not an individual approval by Sulake and not an open-content license. The official practical guidance permits responsible use of publicly accessible Sulake-owned images under fansite restrictions. EN policy 3.1 permits anyone to create a compliant fansite without automatically receiving official status; BR 1.1 uses wording referring to registered fansites. Record that wording difference rather than claiming unconditional authorization.

Use public, released Sulake sprites as noncommercial editorial museum specimens, with exact image URL, access date, original dimensions, identifiers and ownership attribution. Keep provenance and credits of source archives separately: the archive supplying a file does not thereby own or relicense Habbo artwork. Third-party art, photographs, edits or composite screenshots may involve additional rights; do not apply Sulake's policy blanketly to them. Exclude unreleased/leaked material.

Both policy versions retain Sulake ownership, make permission revocable and prohibit implied endorsement. Display the policy-required independent-site disclaimer on every generated page, linked to the policy. Do not use the official fansite logo unless registered. Keep Habbo brands unaltered and avoid copying overall official trade dress or presenting the museum as an official service. Viewing collectible furniture and badge images is distinct from recreating the Habbo service; do not add retro hotel operation or imply items can be won, purchased or transferred here.

Maintain free access and no profit objective, paid access, item sales, credential collection or links to scam/retro/hacking services. Existing project noindex/noarchive measures are editorial precautions and do not create copyright permission. Revisit conditions if monetization, sponsorship, public indexing or account features change. Respect removal requests and keep source assets auditable.

## Publication caveat

Conditional policy basis supports a small curated, attributed, noncommercial museum collection; it does not establish asset authenticity, historical dates, a market value, or general permission to redistribute whole source collections. Preserve uncertainty in each record and do not label a specimen historically verified merely because its image is public. Keep reference-only records excluded when source/ownership/release status cannot be established. Publication remains subject to policy compliance and revocation.
