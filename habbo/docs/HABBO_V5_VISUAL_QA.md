# V5 — visual decisions and evidence

The mandatory comparative captures and two motion recordings are in the PR22 Actions artifact linked from HABBO_V5_QA_REPORT.md. Includes V4 baseline desktop/mobile, V5 dock/groups, rare hero, family rails, badge mural, enlarged specimens, sourced item records and language continuity.

Decision: keep variant A, the persistent compact collection selector. It makes all three worlds discoverable without changing the place dock. Variant B hides the collection links inside an extra disclosure action; it adds interaction cost and is less discoverable. The comparison suite also captures the disclosure open. The selected control remains a small capsule, not a corporate navigation bar.

Rare furniture is displayed on a quiet stage with original proportions, pixel rendering and family rails. Badges remain small symbolic specimens in a grouped mural; the viewer enlarges them and opens the documentary layer. No shopping interface, market prices, card borders or invented historical links were added.

Review boundary: CI renders demonstrate the recorded Chromium viewports, keyboard/touch-sized button interactions and reduced-motion configuration. They do not prove every physical device or every browser. Historical and rights confidence is recorded separately from visual success.
