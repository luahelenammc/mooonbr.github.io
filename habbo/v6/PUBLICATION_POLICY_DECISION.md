# V6 conditional fansite publication record

**Status:** conditional-policy interpretation; **NOT** an individual or official permission from Sulake.

The user requested an actual V6 public delivery. The English [Habbo Fansite Policy](https://help.habbo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011512480-Habbo-Fansite-Policy) section 3.1 allows creation of nonofficial compliant fansites and section 1.1 provides revocable noncommercial fan-site reuse. The [BR/PT policy](https://help.habbo.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/360011610939-Pol%C3%ADtica-de-F%C3%A3-Sites-do-Habbo-Hotel) section 1.1 references 'fã-sites registrados' and the registration process in section 3, creating ambiguity.

This release relies on the **documented, conditional international policy** for an independent noncommercial historical showcase while explicitly acknowledging the ambiguous Portuguese wording. It does NOT say that the project is officially registered, endorsed or separately approved. The policy can be revoked and rights remain with their owners.

Gates required:
- official-source or primary-archive GIF per item;
- exact SHA-256 match with Work V6 manifest;
- each HTML page includes nonaffiliation disclaimer;
- no misleading Habbo trade-dress clone;
- no fees, ads, affiliate sales or client login;
- original images unaltered, no AI images;
- provenance and rights ledger preserved;
- prompt removal if the rights holder objects.

If any item is unavailable or fails the hash, do not fabricate its image or claim it was published.
